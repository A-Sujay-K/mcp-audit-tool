# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility and apologize to those affected by our mistakes.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to `akarshsujaykumar.23is@saividya.ac.in`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
